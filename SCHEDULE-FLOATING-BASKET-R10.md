# Schedule floating Basket R10

- Additional Visit dialog: close X, Cancel, Escape and backdrop click all close reliably.
- Basket is a floating window over the calendar rather than a permanent layout column.
- Basket can be dragged around the viewport.
- Basket can be minimized to its header.
- Basket can be tucked away into a small edge strip and reopened.
- Basket position/state persist locally.
- Calendar keeps the full-width R9 layout and dense busy-day cards.
- Basket contents remain compact: client name + street + suburb.
