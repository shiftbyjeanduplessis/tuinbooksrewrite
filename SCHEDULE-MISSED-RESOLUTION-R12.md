# Schedule missed-visit resolution R12

- Normal-mode visit-card click opens Visit actions; the `i` button remains information-only.
- Visit-specific **Do not service this visit** is prominent in the Visit actions dialog.
- A missed visit presents exactly three primary outcomes: **It was completed**, **Reschedule / catch-up**, **No catch-up / no charge**.
- The original missed visit remains auditable; catch-up creates a linked replacement; no-return is non-billable; completed creates/corrects a Work record.
- The Schedule legend is explicitly a status key, not a set of buttons.
- R/A/Q/Missed/DNS visual markers remain driven by visit state.
