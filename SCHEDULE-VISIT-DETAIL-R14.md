# TuinBooks Schedule R14

- Clicking a visit card opens the actual visit detail first, not a wall of admin actions.
- Shows client/site, street/suburb, team/date/status, service/task list, site instructions, access notes, routine agreement notes and visit notes.
- Uses business service names instead of raw service identifiers where the catalogue has a match.
- Future scheduled visits have only two primary admin actions: Do not service and Cancel visit.
- Do not service then asks whether the scope is this visit or the entire client.
- Cancel then asks charge or no charge.
- Missed visits retain the three-way outcome flow: completed, reschedule/catch-up, no catch-up/no charge.
- Suspend is removed from the primary card-click dialog.
