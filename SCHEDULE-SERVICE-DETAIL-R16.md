# R16 — service detail and Do Not Service toggle

- Card click remains the single primary visit-detail action.
- Work for the visit uses the original TuinBooks visual language again: distinct service icons and compact labelled service chips rather than generic tick circles.
- Specific/custom instructions are separated from service types so the user can scan the job quickly.
- Do Not Service is now a literal two-state control: Service normally / Do not service.
- Choosing Service normally clears every active Do Not Service state currently affecting that visit (visit-specific and client-wide), making the path back to ordinary servicing explicit.
- Choosing Do not service opens scope selection: this visit only or entire client.
- Administrative actions remain secondary to visit scope and instructions.
