# TuinBooks Schedule R13

- Normal mode has one visit-card action surface: click the card to open Visit Actions.
- The `i` indicator is hover/focus information only; the `•••` card menu is removed.
- Rearrange mode adds checkboxes to movable visit cards.
- Tick several cards and drag any selected card to move the selected set together.
- Selected group moves are occurrence-only by design; they do not rewrite future recurrence.
- A single recurring-card move retains the existing This visit / This + future decision.
- Multi-select moves to another calendar cell and to Basket are persisted atomically by R13 RPCs.
- Street address, team colours, drag ID, floating Basket and prior Schedule operations remain.
