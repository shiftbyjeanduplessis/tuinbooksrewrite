# TuinBooks Schedule usability restoration — R7

The fast v2 scheduling engine remains authoritative. This release restores the working TuinBooks Schedule interaction model instead of replacing it.

- Restores an explicit **Drag mode**. Schedule cards are locked for normal use and become draggable only in Drag mode.
- Restores **THIS VISIT / THIS + FUTURE** drag scope controls for recurring work.
- Restores compact **rolling week cards** at the top for fast week navigation.
- Removes Schedule **capacity meters** and safe-hours UI.
- Keeps compact, always-visible **Note** and **Event** buttons.
- Adds a compact **+ Visit** button in every team/day cell for a direct Additional Visit for an existing client. This remains operational extra work, not a quote or invoice workflow.
- Makes client names materially larger and readable while keeping the newer **street address + suburb** directly on each schedule card.
- Keeps the newer visible **drag ID** in the drag ghost.
- Reduces Basket cards to **client name + street address + suburb** only, plus the drag handle.
